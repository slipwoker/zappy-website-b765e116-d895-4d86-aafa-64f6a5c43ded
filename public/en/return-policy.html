Last updated: 26.5.2026

1. Right of Cancellation

In accordance with consumer protection law, you have the right to cancel a transaction within 14 days of receiving the product.

  • For senior citizens, new immigrants and people with disabilities - the cancellation period is 4 months
  • The product must be returned in its original packaging
  • The refund will be processed within 14 days of the cancellation notice

2. Non-Returnable Courses

  • Perishable food products
  • Courses created specifically to order
  • Software and CDs that have been opened
  • Personal hygiene products that have been opened

3. Defective Product

In case of a defective product or one different from what was described, you are entitled to a full refund without cancellation fees, including refund of shipping costs.
